PET CUTTER TROUBLESHOOTING KNOWLEDGE

Equipment:
PET Pellet Cutter

Condition:
Repeated cutter trips with abnormal vibration.

Safety:
Keep the cutter stopped if repeated trips are accompanied
by abnormal vibration until maintenance or engineering
has completed a safe inspection.

Initial checks:
1. Review recent cutter trip alarms.
2. Inspect cutter blade condition.
3. Check cutter bearing temperature.
4. Measure cutter vibration.
5. Check cutter alignment.
6. Check motor current.
7. Inspect for loose mechanical components.

Possible causes requiring verification:
- Blade wear or damage.
- Cutter misalignment.
- Bearing degradation.
- Mechanical looseness.
- Material buildup.
- Motor overload.
- Electrical or control-system fault.

Important:
These possible causes are hypotheses only.
Do not classify them as confirmed failures without
inspection or measurement evidence.

Return to service:
The cutter should only be returned to normal operation
after the identified condition has been inspected,
corrected where required, and safe operation verified.
